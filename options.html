Practice Pro tools 🔒 Practice Pro
Quality checks and review queues for bookkeeping practices.
Approval guardrail — require tracking categories
Blocks Approve on invoices and bills, and OK in bank reconciliation, until every populated line has a tracking category. Xero leaves untracked lines out of MTD quarterly totals.
Approval guardrail — require line descriptions
Blocks Approve when a populated invoice or bill line has no description. Drafts still save normally.
Watch Awaiting Approval queues
Records counts visible on Xero invoice and bill lists, then sends a local Chrome notification when a previously seen count increases.
Find & Recode — show Description index
Adds a searchable A-Z/Z-A review index for the visible results without changing Xero's transaction order.
