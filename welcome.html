Process bank statements without the mouse

If you reconcile regularly, the Bank Reconciliation screen is where you spend most of your time. Normally it's click Match, wait, click OK, scroll to the next line, repeat — dozens of times.

Xero Power adds keyboard shortcuts that let you fly through the list. Each statement line is highlighted in green as you go, so you always know where you are.

How to use it — step by step

1
Open Bank Reconciliation. A green bar appears at the top confirming shortcuts are active. The first line is highlighted.
2
Press M to Match, C to Create, or T to Transfer — whichever applies to that line.
3
If Xero offers you a list of transactions to match against, use ↑ ↓ to move through it and Space to pick each one. Xero shows a tick when the totals agree.
4
Press Enter to reconcile. For a transaction you have created, press Ctrl + Enter to save it.
5
Esc takes you back to the statement lines at any point.

Free · 444 votes

The default Approve button no longer sends email

Xero's out-of-the-box button says "Approve & email" — it fires off an email to your client every single time you approve. One misclick and your client gets an unexpected message. Over 440 users asked Xero to change this. Xero declined.

Xero Power quietly flips the default so the main button just says Approve. The email option isn't gone — it's still in the dropdown (▾) whenever you genuinely need it.

Before — Xero default
Every approval triggers an email — even when you don't want one
After — Xero Power
Approves silently. Click ▾ when you actually want to send email

This works on both Invoices and Bills. You can turn either off in Settings if you ever want the old behaviour back.

Free for two organisations · 308 votes

Know whose books you are in

If you look after several organisations, they all look the same. That is how a transaction ends up in the wrong client's file.

Give each organisation its own navigation bar colour and you know where you are before you read a word. Two are free; Solo Pro and Practice Pro cover as many as you run.

⚠️
There is a subtler version of this. 23 of Xero's screens carry no organisation in their web address — they open whichever organisation your session moved to last. Reach one of those from a different tab and you can land in another client's books with nothing on screen to tell you. Xero Power notices and says so, before you start typing.
Practice Pro · 287 votes

Stop miscoded lines before they land

One untagged line means a report that does not add up, and someone reopening the file weeks later. Xero has said it will not make the field required.

Switch the requirement on and Approve is blocked — on invoices, on bills, and in bank reconciliation — until every populated line carries a tracking category. The lines missing one are marked. You can require a description the same way.

💾
Drafts still save. Only approval is held back, so nobody loses work in progress. It is a guardrail, not a lock — anyone who turns the extension off can approve without it. It is there to catch the honest slip.
📅
If you file under Making Tax Digital: Xero's own guidance is to separate businesses with tracking categories, and a line without one is left out of the quarterly totals sent to HMRC. Xero suggests finding the gaps afterwards with Find & Recode — after the return has gone. Bank reconciliation is where most of those lines come from, which is why the check runs there too.
