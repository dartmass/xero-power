Privacy Policy — Xero Power

Last updated: June 2026

What Xero Power does

Xero Power is a Chrome extension that adds keyboard shortcuts and a command palette to xero.com. It runs entirely inside your browser.

Data we collect

None.

Xero Power does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data about you or your Xero account. Specifically:

  • We do not read your accounting data, transactions, invoices, or contacts.
  • We do not access your Xero credentials or login information.
  • We do not send any information to external servers.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

What the extension does access

The extension reads the structure of Xero web pages (the HTML DOM) solely to inject keyboard shortcut listeners and the command-palette UI into your browser. This is equivalent to what your browser already does when rendering the page — no data leaves your machine.

Permissions

The only browser permission used is access to *.xero.com pages. No other websites are accessed.

Third parties

We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information in the first place.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or email support@xeropower.app.
